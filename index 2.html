Prime Oracle

Type any whole number. The oracle gives an exact verdict and shows where the number sits in the subset binary system.

>
Try
The verdict is exact: Miller-Rabin for any size, trial division to list factors. The subset layer, drawn from EJ’s Prime Number Pattern and Twin Prime Counterflow Model, shows each number’s end-digit code, its slot in a 30-block byte, its mod-9 indicator and its rise/fall skeleton. Its three gates rule numbers out; they can’t confirm a prime on their own.
